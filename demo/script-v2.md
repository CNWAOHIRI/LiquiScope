# LiquiScope — video script v2: "The warning you never got" (≤90s, #OKXAI)

Pain-first cut. One idea per shot, almost no jargon — the only number the viewer
must feel is **$180,000 vs 15 cents**. (v1, the technical cut, stays in script.md.)

Screens: browser (web UI), phone (Telegram), one 5-second terminal beat, marketplace page.

---

## Shot 1 — The pain (0:00–0:18) · web UI

Open the app, click the **Critical example** chip. Red CRITICAL fills the screen.
Slow zoom on the numbers.

> "Every night, somebody wakes up to find their crypto gone. Not hacked — *liquidated*. This is a real wallet, live right now: a hundred and eighty thousand dollars of collateral. If ETH dips three percent — an ordinary Tuesday — it all gets seized, plus a penalty fee. And DeFi never sends a warning letter."

## Shot 2 — The relief (0:18–0:38) · web UI

Click the **Safe example** chip → green. Then back to the critical one. Cursor lands on
the plain-English recommendation text.

> "LiquiScope is that warning. Paste any wallet — it checks every lending position you have, across every major chain, and tells you in plain English: how close you are to the edge, exactly what price triggers it, and what to do about it. Not a dashboard of numbers. An answer."

## Shot 3 — The bodyguard (0:38–0:55) · phone, Telegram

The real alert thread: 🚨 critical alert, the "now monitoring" message.

> "And you don't have to keep checking. LiquiScope watches your wallet around the clock, and the moment your position starts sliding, it messages you — while there's still time to fix it. The liquidation bots are watching your wallet twenty-four seven. Now something's watching your back."

## Shot 4 — The twist (0:55–1:15) · terminal, 5 seconds, then marketplace page

Terminal: the paid report scrolling past (pre-recorded Shot-3-v1 footage works).
Cut to okx.ai/agents/5074.

> "Here's the part that's new: LiquiScope isn't an app you subscribe to. It's an AI agent with a job. It sells this report to other AI agents for fifteen cents, and they pay it automatically, on-chain — no accounts, no cards, no humans in the loop. It's listed on OKX dot AI, the world's first marketplace where AIs hire each other."

## Shot 5 — Close (1:15–1:28) · web UI on the critical wallet

> "Fifteen cents for the warning. Or a hundred and eighty thousand dollars for missing it. LiquiScope — live now on OKX dot AI. Hashtag OKX AI."

## Why this cut works

- v1 sells *how it's built* (402s, signatures, chains) → judges.
- v2 sells *why it exists* (loss aversion, sleep, "nobody warns you") → everyone.
  The tech appears exactly once (Shot 4) and only as "AIs pay each other," which IS
  the hackathon story — protocol names never spoken.
